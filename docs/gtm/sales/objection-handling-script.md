# Objection Handling Script

## Objection 1: “We already have too many tools.”
- Reframe: KronosChamber and KronosCode reduce workflow fragmentation and align execution under one delivery model.
- Follow-up: “Would it be useful to test whether this simplifies your current process in a 2-week pilot?”

## Objection 2: “Closed-source is a risk for us.”
- Reframe: Closed-source here means controlled operations, stable product delivery, and accountable vendor ownership.
- Follow-up: “Let’s run a trust review and define specific requirements during pilot planning.”

## Objection 3: “We are not sure about ROI.”
- Reframe: We define baseline metrics before kickoff and evaluate at week 2 and final review.
- Follow-up: “If we don’t meet agreed success criteria, you do not proceed to rollout.”

## Objection 4: “Our team is too busy to onboard.”
- Reframe: The pilot is scoped to minimal disruption and designed to show impact quickly.
- Follow-up: “Can we start with one high-impact workflow and a small core group?”

## Objection 5: “We need enterprise readiness.”
- Reframe: Enterprise packaging includes trust and procurement support paths.
- Follow-up: “Let’s align required controls and phase rollout accordingly.”
