# Sales and Pilot Scenarios

## Scenario A: Startup Team Without Formal AI Workflow
- Objective: demonstrate baseline workflow acceleration.
- KPI focus: time-to-first-shipped deliverable.
- Checkpoints: week 2 and final review.

## Scenario B: Startup Team Replacing Fragmented Tooling
- Objective: demonstrate reduced process fragmentation.
- KPI focus: execution consistency and adoption rate.
- Checkpoints: week 2 and final review.

## Scenario C: Security-Sensitive Startup Team
- Objective: validate closed-source trust and controlled rollout confidence.
- KPI focus: trust clearance + delivery impact.
- Checkpoints: week 2 and week 4.

## Scoring Framework
- Green: meets or exceeds all agreed pilot success criteria.
- Yellow: partial success with defined remediation path.
- Red: misses critical criteria; do not expand.
