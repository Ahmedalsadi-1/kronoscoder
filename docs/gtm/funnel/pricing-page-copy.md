# Pricing Page Narrative (Tiered SaaS + Team Seats)

## Pricing Philosophy
Price for delivery outcomes and team impact, not raw feature volume.

## Plan Ladder
1. Individual
- Purpose: discovery and personal workflow evaluation
- Positioning: low-friction entry point

2. Team
- Purpose: cross-functional engineering execution
- Positioning: collaboration and governance controls for active teams

3. Enterprise
- Purpose: procurement, security review, and organizational rollout
- Positioning: support, controls, and enterprise readiness

## Messaging Blocks
- “Choose the plan that matches your delivery stage.”
- “Upgrade when your team needs shared control and governance.”
- “Talk to us for enterprise rollout requirements.”
